Bomb Slime

Level Designer & Programmer · JARL (formerly Team Haven), team of five · Unity 6

2D precision platformer, free on itch.io for browser and Windows. A two-semester degree project, from prototype to public release.

  • Level design: authored the original eight-room Level 1 layout on paper, labelling every room with the mechanic it teaches. Ran trap, obstacle and finishing passes on Mine Caves 1 and 3, and built Mine Cave 2's moving platforms, crushers and laser, redesigning its first half where playtesters rated it poorly.
  • Playtesting: ran the 28 October hybrid group playtest (16 of the project's 20 survey responses, 19 opt-in session recordings) and built the WebGL browser build used for the September Alpha playtests.
  • Evidence-led iteration: owned the opening-difficulty risk raised at Alpha, when 3 of 4 testers found the opening too hard. By release, 15 of 20 testers rated Mine Cave 1 easy or just right.
  • Production: 141 logged hours and 72 non-merge commits. Added checkpoints, a capacity pickup, a run timer, the bomb-inventory display and credits.
  • Showcasing: ran the game's station at a Sydney Games Festival showcase (15 Oct 2026).

IAmNesia

Game jam · Leader, Programmer & Designer

A 2D visual novel role-play puzzle. You are Nesia, a girl who wakes up with no memory inside an empty storybook, carrying only a magic pen, and sets out to uncover her past.

What I learned: I tried to build every mechanic I could think of instead of refining one. It didn't place, and it taught me to pick one mechanic and polish it.
