Bomb Slime

Level Designer & Programmer · JARL (formerly Team Haven), team of five · Unity 6

2D precision platformer, free on itch.io for browser and Windows. A two-semester degree project, from prototype to public release. I designed layout-first on paper, built and tuned in-engine, and changed levels based on playtest evidence.

  • Level design: authored the original eight-room Level 1 layout on paper, labelling every room with the mechanic it teaches. Designed and iterated traps and obstacles, and did the final polish, on Mine Caves 1 and 3, and built Mine Cave 2's moving platforms, crushers and laser, redesigning its first half where playtesters rated it poorly.
  • Post-release playtesting: ran the 28 October hybrid group playtest (downloaded build plus itch.io browser build: 16 of the project's 20 survey responses, 19 opt-in session recordings).
  • Evidence-led iteration: owned the opening-difficulty risk raised at Alpha, when 3 of 4 testers found the opening too hard. By release, 15 of 20 testers rated Mine Cave 1 easy or just right.
  • Production: implemented checkpoints, capacity pickups, a run timer, the bomb-inventory display and the credits, and built the WebGL browser build used for the Alpha playtests. Collaborated through Git/GitHub and branch integration.
  • Showcasing: ran the game's station at a Sydney Games Festival showcase (15 Oct 2026).

FOCUS!

Game Jam 2025, Macquarie University
Designer and developer (Unity, C#)

My first game jam. You play an office worker with ADHD trying to survive a gruelling workday: water your plant, avoid your nosy coworker and keep an eye on your boss to stay focused and finish your work. We used the jam's theme of representation to show the everyday struggles of people with ADHD.

What I did: suggested MindMeister for planning the concept and visuals, communicated with every team member to connect the front end and back end, and came up with the original idea for the water-the-plant mini-game.

IAmNesia

Game Jam 2026, Macquarie University
Leader, designer and main developer

A 2D visual novel role-play puzzle. You are Nesia, a girl who wakes up with no memory inside an empty storybook, carrying only a magic pen, and sets out to uncover her past.

What I did: led the team and built most of the game.

What I learned: I tried to build every mechanic I could think of instead of refining one. It didn't place, and it taught me to pick one mechanic and polish it.
